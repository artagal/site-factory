# Site Factory Keyword Clusters

Date: 2026-05-21

Purpose: keyword planning for Site Factory properties and future validation lanes. This is a strategy artifact, not a generated page set.

## Ownership Map

- HVAC: Contactor first; Work Organizer later if service-business operations become a product lane.
- Field service business: Work Organizer first.
- Contractor scheduling: Work Organizer for business operations, Contactor for homeowner project coordination.
- Beauty appointments: future Beauty Drop validation lane only.
- AI creators: GoFunMotion.
- AI models: GoFunMotion.
- Local service businesses: Work Organizer for operators, Contactor for homeowners.

- Homeowners comparing expensive HVAC bids.
- Homeowners preparing questions before estimate appointments.
- Homeowners trying to understand what is included or excluded in HVAC quotes.

- `HVAC estimate checklist`
- `compare HVAC quotes`
- `HVAC contractor questions`
- `HVAC replacement estimate checklist`
- `HVAC quote comparison`

Secondary keywords:

- `questions to ask HVAC contractor`
- `what should be included in HVAC estimate`
- `HVAC bid comparison`
- `furnace replacement estimate checklist`
- `AC replacement quote comparison`

Recommended pages:

1. `/contactor/hvac-estimate-checklist`
2. `/contactor/compare-hvac-quotes`
3. `/contactor/questions-to-ask-hvac-contractor`
4. `/contactor/hvac-replacement-estimate-checklist`
