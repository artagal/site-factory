# What to Track Before Hiring a Home Service Contractor

Hiring a contractor gets easier when every bid is compared against the same set of details. Without that structure, the cheapest estimate can look better than it is, and the most complete estimate can seem expensive for the wrong reason.

Contactor should help homeowners slow the process down just enough to make a confident decision.

## Track the Project Scope

Write down what the contractor is actually agreeing to do. Include room names, measurements, materials, cleanup, permits, and anything that is excluded from the quote.

## Track Proof and Protection

Before hiring, collect:

- License information
- Insurance confirmation
- Warranty terms
- References or past project examples
- Payment schedule
- Change order process

## Compare Bids by Assumptions

Two bids are rarely equal. One may include disposal, upgraded materials, or permit handling. Another may leave those as later costs. A good comparison tool highlights assumptions so the homeowner can ask better questions before signing.
