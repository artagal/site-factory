# BeautyDrop

BeautyDrop is a validation concept for last-minute beauty appointments at a discount. Beauty professionals can post cancellation spots, slow-hour deals, and model-needed appointments. Customers can find same-day and next-day beauty deals nearby.

## Core Promise

Find discounted beauty appointments without booking weeks in advance. BeautyDrop should make open slots easy to understand by showing the service, time, original price, deal price, category, and location area.

## First Audience

The first audience has two sides:

- Customers looking for affordable nails, lashes, brows, hair, facials, makeup, or waxing appointments
- Independent beauty professionals who want to fill empty appointment time without discounting their entire service menu

## Validation Signals

- Clicks on "I want beauty deals"
- Clicks on "I am a beauty professional"
- Requests on specific deal cards
- Provider interest in posting a real open slot
- Category preference across nails, lashes, brows, and hair
- Objections about trust, confirmation, location, timing, and price
