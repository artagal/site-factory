# Beauty Drop

Beauty Drop is a validation concept for curated beauty bundles released around a clear theme, season, or routine. The first landing page should test the promise before building operations.

## Core Promise

Discover a focused beauty drop without sorting through endless product pages. Each drop should feel curated, timely, and easy to understand.

## First Audience

The first audience is beauty-curious shoppers who like discovery but want a smaller, more guided experience than a giant marketplace.

## Validation Signals

- Clicks on a future waitlist action
- Interest in specific drop themes
- Questions about price, delivery, and product selection
- Repeat visits from social or search traffic
